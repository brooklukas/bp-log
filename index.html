History

    Taking an accurate reading
    • Same time each day, before taking your tablet.
    • No caffeine, smoking or exercise for 30 minutes before.
    • Sit with back supported, feet flat, legs uncrossed. Rest 5 minutes.
    • Cuff on bare upper arm, at heart level, arm resting on a table.
    • Don't talk. Take two readings a minute apart and log the second.

    Categories follow the Heart Foundation (Australia) adult guideline. Home readings average a little lower than clinic ones, so the home limit is 135/85. Talk to your GP about what your numbers mean for you.
